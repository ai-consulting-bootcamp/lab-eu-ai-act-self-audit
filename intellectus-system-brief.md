# Intellectus System Brief

Intellectus is a web-based preparation tool for consultants who advise charities, non-governmental organizations, and other social-impact organizations. Before a client conversation, the consultant enters the organization's name, website, country, current challenge, and a research date range. The system uses that information to organize public evidence and prepare a structured starting point for discussion. It does not approve funding, rank people, determine eligibility, or make a final decision for the client.

The system accepts organizational information and public-source material. Its current live request contains the organization name, website, country, challenge, research window, and references to uploaded documents. In the present browser implementation, selected files remain within the session and are not transmitted because a secure server-side upload process has not been implemented. Public research may nevertheless contain incidental personal data, such as names and professional contact details published on an organization's website. A future document-upload function could also receive personal or confidential information, so production use would require controls that the prototype does not yet provide.

Intellectus produces a pre-engagement diagnostic package rather than a score or binding decision. The package can contain an organization snapshot, sources, evidence, findings, unknowns, contradictions, hypotheses, possible diagnoses, recommendations, proposed key performance indicators, questions for client validation, and a 90-day roadmap. The design distinguishes public evidence from inference, hypothesis, unknown information, and recommendation. Baselines and targets remain unconfirmed when evidence is insufficient.

The people most likely to be affected are the client's employees, volunteers, service users, donors, and other stakeholders discussed in the diagnostic. They do not currently interact directly with the system. The immediate user is the consultant, whose interpretation of the output could influence advice given to the client and, indirectly, later organizational decisions.

Human review is intended to occur before any output is used. In the interface, the consultant reviews the diagnostic, selects questions to ask the client, examines a bounded next step and proposed measures, and marks the conversation brief ready. Recommendations can carry a `requires_human_review` flag, and analytical items have review states such as not reviewed, needs review, approved, or rejected. However, the prototype does not yet provide authenticated reviewer identities, durable approval records, mandatory sign-off, or an override log.

The system was designed and built by Paola Hintze and the Project 3 team using a React/TypeScript interface and configured n8n workflows. n8n orchestrates research, evidence handling, analysis, transformation, validation, and response delivery. The repository does not identify a final production AI-model or research-API provider. In production, authorized consultants would operate Intellectus; client staff would receive and discuss the human-reviewed brief rather than act on an unreviewed system output.
