# Gap Analysis — Platform Risk Research Agent

## Obligation checklist note

The Phase 4 obligation checklist applies only to high-risk systems under Annex III. This system was classified as limited risk / transparency in Step 2. It does not fall under any Annex III area and does not make a listed high-risk decision. Because of this, the 11 high-risk provider obligations do not apply, and the checklist table is skipped. The relevant obligation is the Article 50 transparency duty, covered in the gaps below.

## Gap 1

**Obligation:** Article 50, AI-generated content disclosure

**Current state:** The report does not carry any label or notice stating that it was generated by an AI system.

**Required state:** Users must be told clearly that the content they are reading is AI-generated.

**Remediation:** Add a visible label on the report, for example "This report was generated by an AI agent." Place it at the top of the report, not in fine print.

**Escalation needed?** No

## Gap 2

**Obligation:** Evidence source transparency (not a strict AI Act requirement, but relevant under GDPR and consumer protection law)

**Current state:** The confidence tagging system (strong evidence, limited evidence, inferred, no signal found) already separates vendor self-reported claims from independently verified claims.

**Required state:** Users should be able to tell where a claim comes from and how strong it is.

**Remediation:** None needed. This is a strength of the current design. State this in the compliance memo as evidence of good practice, not as a gap.

**Escalation needed?** No

## Gap 3

**Obligation:** Possible Annex III boundary question, recommendation engine

**Current state:** When the verdict is "not recommended," the system ranks and suggests alternative vendors. This goes beyond a neutral score and moves toward decision support.

**Required state:** Unclear. It is not settled whether this ranking behavior counts as "significant influence" on a decision, which would raise the risk tier.

**Remediation:** Get a legal read on whether the recommendation and ranking feature changes the risk tier. If it does, transparency and documentation obligations would need to expand accordingly.

**Escalation needed?** Yes, to a lawyer familiar with AI Act risk tier boundaries
