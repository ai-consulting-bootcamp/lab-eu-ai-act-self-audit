Questions and answers
Does this system fall under any prohibited category (Article 5)?:	No. The system does not do social scoring, biometric categorization, emotion inference, or manipulation. It scores vendors, not people.
Does this system operate in any of the eight Annex III areas?:	No. Annex III covers areas like employment, credit scoring, law enforcement, migration, education, and essential services. Vendor risk assessment is not on this list.
If Annex III: does it "significantly influence" decisions in that area, or is it narrow/preparatory?:	Not applicable. The system does not fall under Annex III.
Does this system interact with end users or generate content requiring disclosure (Article 50)?:	Yes. The system generates a report using AI. The user reads AI-generated content and may act on it. This likely triggers a disclosure duty.
First-pass risk tier:	Limited risk / transparency
One-sentence justification citing the specific article or Annex entry:	The system does not fall under Article 5 prohibited practices or any Annex III high-risk area, but it generates AI-produced content shown directly to a user, which triggers the transparency obligation under Article 50.

Justification paragraph

The system scores AI vendors on trust and risk. It does not make a listed high-risk decision under Annex III. Annex III covers things like hiring, credit, law enforcement, and access to public services. Vendor risk scoring is not one of these areas. The system does not decide anything about a person. It supports a buyer's own decision about a vendor. So it does not qualify as high-risk. But the system does generate content using AI, and a user reads and acts on that content without a human check first. This fits Article 50, which covers AI systems that generate content or interact with users. The user should be told the report is AI-generated. This puts the system in the limited risk / transparency tier, not minimal risk, and not high-risk.
