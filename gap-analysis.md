# Gap analysis and remediation plan: AI Entrepreneur Coach

This system is limited risk, not high-risk, so there is no Phase 4 checklist. Instead, here are the transparency gaps under Article 50, plus one parallel legal issue under GDPR, since the system handles real personal data (CV, personality answers) even though the AI Act itself is not the only law that applies.

I checked the actual code (`app.py`) for these, not just the plan. Right now there is no AI interaction notice, no AI generated content mark, and no privacy notice or data retention logic anywhere in the app.

## Gap 1

- **Obligation:** Article 50(1), tell the user they are talking to an AI system, unless it is already obvious.
- **Current state:** The app is called "AI Entrepreneur Coach," so the name hints at AI, but nothing inside the app itself states clearly that the person is interacting with an AI system before they upload their CV and personality answers.
- **Required state:** A short, plain notice should appear before the user submits their data, saying clearly that this tool uses AI to read their profile and generate suggestions.
- **Remediation:** Add one sentence on the app's first screen, something like: "This tool uses AI (OpenAI) to read your CV and personality answers and suggest business ideas." A few minutes of work, no design changes needed.
- **Escalation needed?** No, this is a simple text addition.

## Gap 2

- **Obligation:** Article 50(2), AI generated content must be marked so a person or a machine can tell it was made by AI.
- **Current state:** The fit explanations and the 90 day roadmap are written entirely by AI (OpenAI), but the exported report (PDF or HTML) does not say anywhere that the text was AI generated.
- **Required state:** The exported report should carry a visible line stating the text was AI generated, and ideally a machine readable tag as well (for example, a small note in the PDF metadata or a marked HTML tag).
- **Remediation:** Add a footer line to every exported report: "This report was generated by AI based on the information you provided. Review it yourself before acting on it." Add the same note in the PDF metadata if the export library supports it.
- **Escalation needed?** No, this is also an implementation fix, not a legal question.

## Gap 3

- **Obligation:** Not from the AI Act itself, but GDPR Article 13, people must be told what personal data is collected, why, and what happens to it.
- **Current state:** The app collects a CV or LinkedIn PDF and personality quiz answers, sends this data to OpenAI, Cohere, and Pinecone (all outside the EU) for processing, and there is no privacy notice, no statement about how long the data is kept, and no way in the app to ask for it to be deleted.
- **Required state:** GDPR requires clear information about what data is collected, why, which third parties (OpenAI, Cohere, Pinecone) process it, whether it leaves the EU, how long it is kept, and how someone can ask for it to be deleted.
- **Remediation:** Add a short privacy notice before upload, listing what is collected, that OpenAI/Cohere/Pinecone process it, and how long it is kept (or state clearly if nothing is stored after the session ends). Also decide and document an actual retention rule, for example "your CV and answers are only used during your session and are not saved."
- **Escalation needed?** Yes, to a data protection specialist or lawyer. Sending EU personal data to US based processors like OpenAI, Cohere, and Pinecone raises international data transfer questions under GDPR that go beyond what a first-pass audit like this one can settle on its own.
