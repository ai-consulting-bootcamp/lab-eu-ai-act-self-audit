# Model Intake Brief compliance memo

**To** Head of Product, prospective EU client  
**From** Sergii Rostovskyi, AI consultant and prototype author  
**Date** 16 November 2026  
**Subject** Conditions for an internal Model Intake Brief pilot

Model Intake Brief falls in the lab's **limited-risk / transparency** tier for the proposed EU deployment. It creates new assessments from public sources, so the provider should plan for AI interaction and output transparency under Article 50. The workflow supports model procurement and has no identified high-risk use.

If I supply the tool under my name, I would be the provider of the finished system. Your company would be the deployer when its staff use it. OpenAI supplies the model that assesses the evidence. Confirm the system-provider role if your company will release or commission the tool under its own name.

Three issues matter before a live pilot.

- **Users and recipients need to know where AI was used.** The live screen mentions OpenAI calls and the report says it is informational. The exported report carries no verified machine-readable marking for the generated findings.
- **Public documents can contain personal information.** A source excerpt sent for assessment or copied into an export can include a contributor's details. The client needs to confirm how that information may be used, shared, and retained.
- **A reviewer can download a brief before checking it.** Evidence statuses varied in an earlier test. A missing result can reflect the search or assessment, so it cannot establish that a supplier lacks the evidence.

First, have legal confirm the release arrangement, whether any narrow marking exception applies, and which notices are required. Ask privacy counsel or the data protection officer to confirm vendor terms, data transfers, and retention. Next, have engineering add the interaction notice, test output marking on the live display and exports, and filter unnecessary personal details before sharing them. Before a live pilot, train the governance reviewers and require a recorded source check and a separate human decision before anyone relies on a brief for integration.

This memo provides a preliminary consulting assessment. Formal legal advice, conformity assessment, and certification are outside its scope. See the [assessment and remediation plan](model-intake-eu-ai-act-assessment.md) for the evidence and proposed checks.
