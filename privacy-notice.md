# Privacy notice (draft)

*This is a draft, meant to close Gap 3 from gap-analysis.md. It should be reviewed by someone with GDPR knowledge before going live, this is a starting point, not a final legal document.*

**What we collect**
Your CV or LinkedIn PDF, your answers to the personality quiz, and the budget and time you enter.

**Why we collect it**
To match you with business ideas that fit your background, personality, and constraints, and to write your personalized report.

**Who else sees it**
Your data is sent to OpenAI (to read your CV and write your report) and Cohere (to help search for matching business ideas). Both are based outside the EU. We do not sell your data or use it for anything other than generating your report.

**How long we keep it**
Your data is used only for your session and is not stored after your report is generated. [Confirm this matches the actual code before publishing, if data is stored anywhere, list that here instead.]

**Your rights**
You can ask us what data we have about you, ask us to delete it, or ask us questions about this notice, by contacting [add contact email]. Since your data is not stored after your session, there is usually nothing left to delete once your report is generated.

**One more thing**
Your report is written by AI based on the information you give us. Please review it yourself before acting on it.
