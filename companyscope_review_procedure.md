# Proposed CompanyScope report review procedure

**Status:** Draft for pilot implementation, not an existing workflow control.

**First screen notice:** “CompanyScope uses AI to draft company research from registry and web sources. Verify the sources before relying on the report. Do not enter confidential client information or personal identifiers.”

**Report banner:** “AI-assisted draft. Web findings are unverified leads. Check the underlying sources before relying on or sharing this report.”

Before a report leaves the research team, the assigned reviewer checks the registered name, court and number against the source. They open the source for every material or adverse claim, confirm it concerns the same entity, check that it supports the statement and distinguish fact from inference. They remove unsupported claims and unnecessary personal data or mark the report **Hold** where evidence is unresolved. A second reviewer checks material adverse findings before any approved external release.

Record the report ID, reviewer, date, outcome (release, revise or hold), source overrides and rationale in a restricted audit record. Do not paste raw research snippets into that record. Apply the retention period verified by the privacy owner. Verify the release copy retains its provenance controls separately from this human review. Escalate any new publication purpose or consequential assessment of individuals to legal.
