# Draft Intellectus Transparency Notice

## Notice shown at first use

**Intellectus uses automated and, where configured, AI-supported processing to prepare a draft diagnostic from the information you provide and available public sources.** It can generate summaries, hypotheses, recommendations, questions, proposed measures, and roadmap actions. These outputs may be incomplete or incorrect and must not be treated as verified facts or final professional advice.

Intellectus distinguishes public evidence from inference, hypothesis, unknown information, and recommendation. A human consultant must review the cited sources, confirm important information with the client, correct or reject unsupported content, and approve the brief before it is used or shared. The system does not make decisions for the client and must not be used to determine a person's employment, education, access to essential services, credit, insurance, legal status, or other rights.

Do not enter special-category personal data, confidential client documents, or person-level case information unless an approved secure process and lawful basis have been established. Public sources may still contain incidental personal information; report inappropriate or unnecessary personal data to the designated reviewer.
