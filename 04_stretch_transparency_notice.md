# Stretch – Transparency Notice for Users (closes Gaps 1 and 2)

*Shown on the input form and linked from every output.*

**This tool uses artificial intelligence.**
When you submit a job link and your CV, an AI model from a third-party provider reads them and drafts three things for you: an interview prep brief, suggestions for your CV, and a cover letter.

**What you should know**
- **Everything it produces is a draft written by AI.** It can be wrong, and it can include details that aren't in your CV. Check every sentence before you use it. You're responsible for what you send to an employer.
- **The cover letter is marked as AI-generated** in the file's metadata.
- **What we send to the AI model:** your work history, education and skills. Before the AI step we remove your photo, date of birth, address and nationality.
- **Where your data goes:** our AI provider processes it under a data-processing agreement and does not use it to train its models. We don't keep your CV after your results are delivered. Workflow logs are deleted automatically after [X days].
- **What this tool does not do:** it doesn't send applications, contact employers, or score or rank you as a candidate.
