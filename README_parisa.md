## First-pass conclusion

TestVet is not prohibited under Article 5 and is not high-risk under Annex III. For this exercise, it is assessed conservatively as **limited risk / transparency**, subject to legal confirmation that its deterministic rule-based workflow qualifies as an AI system under Article 3(1).
