# EU AI Act audit — RegGuard-Mini (Onyx Dynamics)

Module 7 lab submission. First-pass EU AI Act compliance assessment of a system I built:
**RegGuard-Mini**, an assistant that checks vehicle exterior geometry against regulatory and
internal design rules and answers a designer's questions about the result.

## Scope and caveats

- This is a first-pass self-assessment by the system's own designer, not a legal opinion,
  conformity assessment, or certification.
- Classification was formed independently before cross-checking against the European
  Commission's EU AI Act Compliance Checker, as the lab requires.
- Legal references are to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Where I am unsure of a legal
  interpretation or of the current application timeline, the document says so explicitly
  rather than asserting a conclusion.
- No API keys, tokens, or customer geometry are contained in this repository.
