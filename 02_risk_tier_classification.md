## Rationale

The system is classified as **limited risk under Article 50** because it generates AI content and interfaces with end users in a way that may not be immediately apparent to them. This is a transparency obligation, not a high-risk obligation.

The key distinction is that this is a **candidate-side tool for self-presentation**, not an **employer-side hiring system**. Article 50 requires disclosure when AI generates or manipulates content that end users interact with, but does not impose the same level of governance as Annex III high-risk systems that actually make decisions affecting individuals.

No ambiguity remains between risk tiers. The system clearly does not fall into prohibited or high-risk categories, and it clearly triggers Article 50 transparency requirements.
