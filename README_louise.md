## First-pass conclusion

**Limited risk / transparency.** The product does not fall within
Article 5 prohibited practices or Annex III high-risk use cases. Its
direct AI interaction triggers an Article 50 transparency analysis. The
main additional production concerns identified are
GDPR/data-minimisation issues around public YouTube comments and clearer
output provenance.
